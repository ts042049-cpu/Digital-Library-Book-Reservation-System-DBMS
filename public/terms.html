Section 01

Institutional Scope & Membership Eligibility

Statutory

The K.R. Mangalam University Central Library serves as the premier academic information center supporting undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral research, and faculty scholarship across the School of Engineering & Technology (SoET), Management, Legal Studies, and Medical Sciences.

Eligible Cardholders include:

  • Undergraduate & Postgraduate Students: Formally admitted students registered with a valid University Enrollment Roll Number.
  • Faculty Members: Regular and adjunct academic professors, associate professors, and visiting teaching faculty.
  • Research Scholars: Ph.D. and M.Tech research fellows under sponsored or university grant programs.
  • University Officers & Technical Staff: Administrative and lab staff with institutional staff codes.
Automatic Catalog Sync: Membership accounts are synchronized with the university registrar database upon commencement of each academic semester.
Section 02

Membership & Smart ID Card Regulations

Strict Enforcement

Circulation desk transactions (issuing, returning, renewing, and fine assessment) require the physical or digital presentation of the institutional Smart ID Card.

  1. Non-Transferability: A member's library card is non-transferable. Borrowing books on behalf of peers or using another student's barcode is strictly prohibited and constitutes academic misconduct.
  2. Lost ID Cards: Loss of an ID card must be reported immediately to the Central Library circulation desk to flag the account and prevent fraudulent checkouts.
  3. Card Replacement: Replacement smart cards carry a university administrative reissue fee of ₹200 via the Registrar's portal.
  4. Address & Contact Updates: Members must ensure their registered email address and mobile phone number are kept up-to-date in the university portal to receive automatic due date reminders.

Section 04

Overdue Fines & Penalty Schedule

Automated SQLite Calc

Books returned after their scheduled due date disrupt catalog circulation. Fines are calculated automatically by the database on a calendar-day basis (including weekends and university breaks).

  • Standard Late Fee: ₹5.00 per day per book past the due date.
  • Extended Default (Day 15+): Escalates to ₹10.00 per day thereafter until the volume is physically checked in.
  • Automatic Account Suspension: Members with pending fines exceeding ₹100.00 or loans overdue by 14+ days will have their borrowing privileges automatically suspended.
  • Fine Payment Methods: Fines can be paid directly at the circulation counter via UPI, Debit Card, or added to semester fee dues.

Section 05

Book Renewal & Reservation Queue Policy

Self-Service

Cardholders can reserve currently issued volumes or renew their active loans through the LMS online catalog desk or in person.

  1. Renewals: Books may be renewed once for an additional 14 days, provided the book has not been reserved by another member and has no accrued overdue fines.
  2. Hold Queue Quota: A maximum of 2 simultaneous book holds may be active per student cardholder.
  3. Hold Shelf Window: Once a reserved volume is returned, the reserving member will receive an automated notification. The volume will be held at the Circulation Hold Shelf for 3 working days before being released to the next member in queue.
Section 06

Lost, Damaged & Defaced Material Policy

Strict Liability

Borrowers are personally responsible for the physical condition of all materials checked out under their account. Volumes should be inspected before leaving the circulation desk.

  • Defacing & Marking: Underlining, highlighting, folding page corners (dog-earing), or tearing book leaves is strictly forbidden.
  • Lost Book Settlement Options:
    1. Physical Replacement: Provide an identical or newer publisher edition of the lost volume in brand new condition, plus a ₹100 processing and RFID re-tagging surcharge.
    2. Financial Reimbursement: Pay the current market replacement price of the book as determined by the University Librarian, plus a 20% institutional acquisition surcharge.
Section 07

Digital Library, Computer Lab & Wi-Fi Usage

IT Governance

The Digital Library & Computer Lab provides high-speed campus internet access and subscriptions to premier global research repositories including IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, and DELNET.

Acceptable Use Regulations:

  • Digital terminals are dedicated exclusively to academic research, coursework, and thesis preparation.
  • Mass scraping or automated bulk downloading of proprietary publisher journals is prohibited under publisher licensing agreements.
  • Gaming, cryptocurrency mining, viewing offensive content, or installing unauthorized software on lab terminals is strictly forbidden.
Section 08

Reading Hall Conduct & Silence Zones

Campus Conduct

To foster an optimal study environment for all university members, adherence to the following decorum rules is mandatory:

  • Silence Zones: First and Second Floor Reading Halls are designated as Absolute Silence Zones. Group discussions are permitted exclusively in Ground Floor Collaborative Pods.
  • Mobile Phones: Mobile devices must be placed on silent mode. Voice calls must be taken outside library premises.
  • Food & Beverages: Food, snacks, and open beverages are strictly prohibited inside library halls to safeguard physical books and electronics. Only sealable water bottles are permitted.
Section 09

Student Data Privacy & Information Security Charter

Privacy Shield

K.R. Mangalam University Central Library is committed to safeguarding student and faculty personal data. This charter outlines our data collection, storage, and confidentiality practices.

1. Data Collected by the LMS:

  • Identity Profile: Full Name, Student/Employee ID, Institutional Email, Contact Phone, and Department.
  • Circulation History: Books issued, due dates, return timestamps, renewal counts, and fine receipts.
  • System Audit Trails: Timestamped records of administrative actions (e.g., book catalog additions, card status updates) to prevent tampering.

2. Information Protection Standards:

  • Staff credentials are protected using salted cryptographic hashing (bcrypt).
  • Circulation databases employ parameterized SQL queries to eliminate SQL injection vulnerabilities.
  • Session cookies utilize HTTP-only protections with 24-hour expiration.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: Library data is never leased, sold, or shared with commercial advertising entities.

3. Student Rights:

Members have the right to inspect their complete borrowing history, review accrued fines, and request corrections to their contact profiles via the circulation desk or online student portal.
